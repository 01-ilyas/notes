Panel Discussion on Modern Parenting in AI Era

Panelist One

  • Was able to spend quality time with her daughter.
  • Considers herself as a patient parent.
  • Servants, Grand parents and others present in house contribute to parenting and growth of children.
  • Looking back she thinks she should have been more firmer and had set definite boundaries.

Panelist Two

  • She believes parents should be emotionally available for children.
  • She does gentle parenting and her husband is an advocate of traditional parenting.
  • Children may try to trick us and try to take advantage of our emotional vulnerability as we are also learning and evolving in our role as parents.
  • Thinking from the shoes all the people may introduce stress in making decisions regarding parenting.
  • As parents we are also learning and trying to be a better parent.

Panelist Three

  • We dont have to be unnecessarily concerned about parenting as this is something that was done naturally from time immemorial.
  • Be a relaxed parent.
  • Support Children when they are in need of support.
  • Parenting is being judged as never before, which is not good.
  • Wrong message given by parenting classes.

Panelist Four

  • Believes in influential parenting
  • Try to be a democratic parent
  • Teach by doing things instead of preaching
  • He doesn’t advocate helicopter parenting
  • Managing anger is one area he wants to improve
  • Unfairness in parenting.

Panelist Five

  • Try to convice and guide his Children.
  • Spending quality time with children.
